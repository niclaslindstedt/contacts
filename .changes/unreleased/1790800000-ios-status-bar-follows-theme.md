type: Fixed
title: The iOS status bar stays readable on every theme
doc: native-app
---

The clock and battery took their colour from the phone's light or dark setting rather than the app's theme, so a dark theme on a phone in light mode drew dark icons on a dark background, and a light theme in dark mode the reverse. Now that the page runs under the status bar, its icons follow the theme the app shows — light on a dark theme, dark on a light one — and switch with it.
