---
type: Removed
title: Google Drive sync
---

Google Drive is no longer one of the places an address book can be kept. On
the website and in the desktop app, this device, a folder you pick and Dropbox
remain; the App Store app keeps this device and iCloud Drive.

If you had Drive connected, the app falls back to this device. Your contacts,
their photos and their attachments stay in their folder in your own Drive,
where you can read, export or delete them as you would any other file —
nothing is removed from your account.
